Emoji encoder

Hide a message inside a single emoji, or spell it out in emoji. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

Carrier emoji

Each bar is one hidden byte, taller for higher values. Amber bars come from characters outside plain ASCII, like é or 🎉.

Some apps strip invisible characters when you paste, which erases the message. If the person you send it to sees only the emoji, try another app.

How it works

Unicode has 256 variation selectors, invisible characters meant to pick between styles of the same symbol. Your message is turned into UTF-8 bytes, each byte becomes one of those selectors, and they’re attached right after the carrier emoji. Most apps show only the emoji but keep the hidden characters when you copy and paste.

Spelling mode uses a fixed alphabet of 256 emoji instead, one per byte, so the result is visible and anyone can decode it here. Neither method is encryption, so don’t use them for secrets that matter.
